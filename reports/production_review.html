Key findings

+12.9%proven uplift from scale-squeeze treatment
  1. Unexplained (not logged)s are the biggest single cause of lost oil (23%). Unplanned causes make up 83% of all deferment.
  2. 23% of lost oil was never explained (166 downtime events missing from the log). You cannot fix what is not recorded, so a logging-discipline fix is the cheapest win.
  3. 5 wells cause 37% of unplanned deferment. Focus reliability reviews there first.
  4. 3 wells have dropped well below their decline forecast in the last 3 months. Investigate them now, before they fail.
  5. The scale-squeeze campaign worked: +12.9% more oil than comparable untreated wells (95% CI +11.1% to +14.8%), about 465 bopd extra. A simple before/after comparison would have said only +8.5%, because it ignores natural decline.

4 · Did the treatment campaign work?

Method: difference-in-differences against 22 untreated wells; Welch t-test p = 3e-09, Mann-Whitney p = 1.7e-07. Indicative value ≈ $8.3M per year with a 30% haircut for fading effect. Recommend extending the campaign and re-measuring at 6 months.

Assumptions

Potential = latest monthly well-test rate. Deferment = potential × hours offline / 24. Oil price $70/bbl. Data is synthetic; methods transfer directly to real daily production reports and downtime logs. Lift-type reliability (mean days between unplanned events): Gas lift 34 d, Natural flow 38 d, ESP 41 d.
